01 / Understanding art

Art gives shape to the things we feel.

Art is a way people express ideas, emotions, experiences, and ways of seeing the world. It can take many forms, including painting, sculpture, photography, music, design, performance, and digital creations.

Art does not only belong inside museums. We can encounter it in our homes, streets, clothing, celebrations, nature, and everyday experiences. It can help us communicate, remember, question, connect, and see ordinary things differently.

“Art helps us notice meaning in the world around us.”
02 / Why art matters

Functions of art

Art can serve different purposes at the same time. These are some common ways art functions in people's lives and communities.

01

Personal

Allows individuals to express feelings, identity, imagination, and personal experiences.

02

Social

Brings people together, communicates shared concerns, and encourages conversation.

03

Cultural

Preserves traditions, stories, beliefs, and the identity of a community.

04

Practical

Combines creativity and usefulness in objects, spaces, architecture, and design.

05

Aesthetic

Creates visual or sensory experiences that can inspire, move, or delight us.

03 / Art in the Philippines

Art galleries and museums give people a chance to encounter artworks, learn about artists, and explore different stories and perspectives.

Manila, Philippines

04 / The group's exhibition

HOME AS...

Seven interpretations. One shared theme. Each artwork offers a different way of understanding what home can mean.

Home does not only refer to a physical house. It can also be found in the people we love, the memories we keep, the places that comfort us, and the experiences that shape who we are. Through this exhibition, our group explores how one familiar word can hold many different meanings.
